# Student Performance Analysis 📊

## Project Overview
This Excel project analyzes student performance to identify the factors associated with final exam scores and provide actionable recommendations to support student achievement.

## Business Problem
How can student academic performance be improved, and what factors are associated with higher final exam scores?

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Excel Formulas
- Correlation Analysis
- Data Visualization

## Dataset
The dataset contains 1,000 student records and includes variables such as:

- Study Time
- Attendance
- Sleep Hours
- Internet Access
- Extracurricular Activities
- Part-Time Job
- Parental Education
- Previous Grade
- Final Exam Score
- Final Grade
- Gender
## Data Source

The dataset used in this project was obtained from Kaggle:

**Student Performance & Study Habits Dataset** by Harshada Patil

[View the original dataset on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/harshadapatil31/student-performance-and-study-habits-dataset)

The dataset was used for educational and portfolio purposes. The data cleaning, analysis, PivotTables, dashboard design, insights, and recommendations were completed as part of this project.

## Key Findings
- The average final exam score is **83.5**.
- **63.8%** of students achieved grades A or B.
- Study time has a moderate positive correlation with final exam scores (**r = 0.6**).
- Attendance has a weak positive correlation with final exam scores (**r = 0.3**).
- Sleep hours have a very weak positive correlation with final exam scores (**r = 0.1**).
- Students with internet access scored approximately **5 points higher** on average.
- Students with part-time jobs scored approximately **3.3 points lower** on average.
- Previous grades have a positive correlation with final exam scores (**r = 0.4**).
- Gender shows almost no meaningful difference in average final exam performance.

## Grade Performance

## Recommendations
- Encourage students with lower scores to increase their study time.
- Improve internet access for students who currently lack reliable access.
- Provide additional support for students with low attendance.
- Consider flexible academic support or study schedules for students who have part-time jobs.
